Threads Has No Download Button — Here's Why That Matters

The Save button isn't a download

Threads doesn't currently have a download button for videos. The Save option is a bookmark — it adds the post to a private list inside Threads so you can find it again, but it doesn't put a file on your device.

That leaves two ways to get a copy: screen recording, or a downloader.

Screen recording vs a downloader

Screen recording works on any phone or computer, but it captures everything on the display — notification banners and the Threads interface included — and the result is only as sharp as your screen resolution.

A downloader gives you the video file attached to the post, at the resolution Threads has for it, with nothing around it.

Video Quality, Format and Watermarks

Quality

You get the resolution Threads served for that post. ThreadsGrab doesn't upscale or re-compress, so a low-resolution upload can't be made sharper. If an HD copy exists, it appears in the list when Threads has one for that video.

Format

MP4, which plays on phones, tablets and computers without extra software. Threads GIFs also save as MP4 — that's how Threads stores them.

Watermark

ThreadsGrab adds no logo or watermark. Anything the poster built into the video — captions, a username or a logo — stays in the file as part of the original.

The tool works with public post URLs from the Threads app, threads.com, and threads.net — both domains are supported, and threads.net links redirect to threads.com automatically.

Saving Threads Videos on iPhone, Android and Desktop

Video files are much larger than photos. On a mobile connection, switch to Wi-Fi for anything longer than a few seconds.

iPhone and iPad. Open ThreadsGrab in Safari, paste the link and tap Download. Safari may offer Save Video, which puts the clip straight into Photos. If the file lands in the Files app instead, open it, tap the share icon and choose Save Video.

Android. The video saves to either a Movies folder or a Downloads folder depending on your browser and file manager. If it doesn't show up in your gallery, check both.

Windows, Mac and Linux. The video saves like any other download to your browser's downloads folder, and the browser shows progress while a longer clip writes to disk. To move it to your phone, use AirDrop, a cloud drive or email.

Troubleshooting Threads Video Downloads

Work through these in order. Most failures end at the first or second check.

Nothing happens, or it stays on "Processing"
Share menus and phone copy-paste sometimes drop the end of a link. Copy it again, paste the full link, reload this page and retry.
A "no media" message, but the post clearly has a video
Open the link in a private window without logging in. If you hit a login wall or blank page, the post is private or restricted — ThreadsGrab sees the same thing.
Nothing returned for a link that opens fine in Threads
Check if it's a reply, quote, profile, or feed page. Open the original post that contains the video and copy that link instead.
The post exists but has only text or a picture
There's no video to save. Link-preview cards don't count. For photos, use the image tool.
It worked before and fails now
The post may have been deleted or edited, or the link is old. Open the post in Threads again and copy a fresh link.
The download starts but you can't find the file
Your browser may have blocked it, or storage may be full. Allow downloads for this site, free some space, then check Downloads — and Movies on Android.
A fresh link to a public post still fails
Threads changes link formats occasionally, and an outage or rate limit can also cause this. Wait a moment and retry. If it still fails, send us the link (public posts only) and what you saw.
