How to Use the Threads Reel Downloader

Three steps: copy the video post link, preview the available file, and save it.

Copy the Threads post link

In the Threads app, open the post, tap Share, then choose Copy link. On the web, copy the post address. A profile link will not work.

Paste the link and load the video

Paste the post link above and select Download. The preview shows the video files Threads makes available for that post.

Choose a video and save it

If more than one video or resolution is listed, choose the version you want. Save it through your browser's usual download prompt.

Downloading a Short Video from Threads

Use this Threads reel downloader to save a short video from a public Threads post. Paste the post link to see the video options; this Threads video downloader does not accept Instagram Reel links. You do not need to enter your Threads password, but the post must be visible without signing in.

Copy the Link to the Video Post

In the Threads app, open the post with the clip, tap Share, and choose Copy link. On the web, open the post and copy its address from the browser. Both threads.com and threads.net post links are supported.

Use the post address, not the creator's profile or your feed. A post URL usually includes a path such as /@username/post/ followed by a code. If you paste a link to a profile, the downloader cannot tell which video you want.

Video File, Format, and Quality

After the post loads, preview the video and choose from the versions or resolutions Threads makes available. The saved file is usually MP4. The resolution depends on the original upload; choosing a higher option cannot restore detail that was not in the source. ThreadsGrab does not add a watermark, though captions or logos already in the video remain.

Animated posts may be served as short videos and save as MP4 rather than GIF. A photo-only post has no video to download. For those, use the Threads image downloader for one photo or the Threads carousel downloader for a multi-photo post.

Where Your Download Goes

Your browser controls the save prompt and file location. On a computer, check its Downloads folder. On Android, look in the browser's downloads list or your file manager; a video may not appear in the gallery straight away. On iPhone or iPad, follow Safari's prompt and check the Files app. If a browser opened inside another app blocks the save, open ThreadsGrab in Safari or Chrome instead.

Long or high-resolution videos use more storage and mobile data. A Wi-Fi connection can help when saving a larger file.

When a Threads Video Will Not Download

First check that the link opens the exact post containing the video and that the post is public and still available. A profile link, a photo-only post, or a removed or restricted post will not provide a video to save. If the preview loads but the download does not start, look for a browser prompt and check the Downloads location.

Before Reposting a Saved Video

Saving a public video does not give permission to repost or reuse it. Keep the creator's attribution and ask before sharing it elsewhere or using it commercially. ThreadsGrab is an independent service and is not affiliated with Threads or Meta.
