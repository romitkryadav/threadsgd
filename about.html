About ThreadsGrab

About Us

A fast, free, privacy-first utility built for Threads creators and users. Download videos, images, DPs, and track follower counts — all without logging in.

What is ThreadsGrab?

Threads makes it easy to share our thoughts with the world, check out profiles, and see what people think. However, sometimes we come across posts we want to save—like videos and images—or we feel curious to track follower counts for our favorite celebrities, friends, or even our own personal accounts.

Unfortunately, Threads does not natively offer these features. That is why we built ThreadsGrab: to fill the gap with a simple, privacy-first set of tools that make it easy to save public Threads content and monitor follower growth.

After spending a lot of time on Threads, we noticed users frequently want a way to download Threads videos and images, view profile photos in full quality, and keep an eye on follower counts. ThreadsGrab was designed to solve that problem and simplify the experience for everyone.

Today ThreadsGrab offers four main tools — Video Downloader, Image Downloader, DP Downloader, and Follower Counter — plus the informational and legal pages on this site.

Our principles

  • No login ever. We will never ask for your Threads or Meta password, auth token, or 2FA code.
  • No watermarking. Downloaded media is delivered exactly as Threads publishes it publicly.
  • No storage of your downloads. Media passes through stateless workers; we don't keep files on our servers.
  • Public content only. If a post or profile is private, we simply do not process it.
