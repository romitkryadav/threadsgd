These Are Unicode Characters, Not Installed Fonts

The generator substitutes Unicode characters that resemble styled letters. It does not install a font or change Threads' typeface. The result may look different across devices, and some characters may not be supported everywhere. Screen readers can pronounce these symbols differently, and search may not recognize stylized letters the same way as ordinary text.

Keep Important Text Easy to Read

Decorative lettering can make a short phrase stand out, but it can also make names, links, or longer descriptions harder to read or find. Consider using plain text for essential details and adding a style only where it helps. After copying, paste the text into Threads and check the preview on your device before saving or posting.

Your Text Stays in the Browser
