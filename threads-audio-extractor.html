Extract Audio from a Public Threads Video

Want to save the sound from a Threads video? Paste the link to its public post into the Threads audio extractor, preview the clip, then select Download Audio (MP3). The result is an audio-only MP3 file, so it is useful when you want to listen to a clip without keeping its video.

Once you start extraction, the video plays in your browser while the page captures and converts its audio track. Processing can take about as long as the video, so leave the tab open until the MP3 download begins. No Threads account sign-in is required.

Use the Link to the Video Post

In Threads, open the post with the video, tap its share control, and choose Copy link. Paste that post URL here; profile links, replies, and links to unrelated pages do not identify the video to process. The tool accepts public Threads post links from threads.com and threads.net.

The post must be publicly viewable and contain a video with an audio track. A private or removed post cannot be retrieved, and a photo-only post has no video soundtrack to extract. ThreadsGrab does not sign in to accounts or bypass privacy settings.

Choose an MP3 Bitrate

After the video loads, select an available bitrate: 96, 128, 192, or 320 kbps. A lower bitrate usually creates a smaller file; a higher bitrate creates a larger file. The selected bitrate controls the MP3 encoding and cannot restore detail that is missing from the original audio.

The MP3 contains the sound that is present in the video, including speech, music, and background noise. This tool does not isolate a song or remove unwanted sounds. If the video is silent or has no audio track, there is no sound to extract.

Browser Processing and Troubleshooting

The browser processes the audio after the public video is loaded. Keep the page open while the clip plays and the MP3 is prepared. Longer clips take longer and may use more device memory, so a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari is recommended.

If processing fails, check that the link opens to a public video post and try again with a shorter clip. Some posts may be unavailable, silent, or encoded in a format your browser cannot play. Refreshing the page can help if the MP3 encoder did not load.

Save and Use Audio Responsibly

The finished MP3 is downloaded through your browser. ThreadsGrab does not ask for your Threads password. Only save or reuse audio when you have the rights or permission to do so, and follow the creator's terms and applicable copyright rules.
