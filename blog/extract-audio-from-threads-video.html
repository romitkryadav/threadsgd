MP3 audio guide

How to Extract Audio from a Threads Video as MP3

The Threads Audio Extractor can save the soundtrack of a public video post as an MP3. Follow these steps, choose a bitrate, and keep the page open while it processes.

Guide by ThreadsGrab · Updated November 20, 2026

What the Threads Audio Extractor does

The tool takes the audio track from a public Threads video post and prepares an MP3 file. It is useful when you want to listen to speech, music, or other sound without keeping the video. It does not identify a song, separate a speaker from background music, or remove noise—the MP3 contains the sound present in the video.

Before starting: the post must be publicly viewable and contain a video with an audio track. A photo-only post, a silent clip, or a post that cannot be opened publicly has no extractable soundtrack for this tool.

Extract audio from a Threads video

  1. Copy the video post link. Open the post in Threads and use its share control to copy the URL. Use the post link rather than the creator’s profile, a reply, or your feed.
  2. Load the video. Go to the Threads Audio Extractor, paste the link, and select Download to load the preview. Check that the preview is the clip you meant.
  3. Choose an MP3 bitrate. Select one of the available options: 96, 128, 192, or 320 kbps. The choice affects how the MP3 is encoded and how large the file is likely to be.
  4. Start extraction. Select Download Audio (MP3). Keep the page open while the browser plays the video and prepares the audio file. When the download starts, use your browser’s normal save prompt.

Choosing between 96, 128, 192, and 320 kbps

A lower bitrate generally creates a smaller MP3 file, which can be useful when storage or data is limited. A higher bitrate generally creates a larger file and may preserve more of the available audio detail during encoding. The source still sets the ceiling: selecting 320 kbps cannot restore detail missing from the video’s audio track.

For spoken clips, a lower or middle option may be sufficient for casual listening. For music or archiving a sound you are allowed to keep, you may prefer a higher option. If you are unsure, compare the file size and listen to the result on the device you plan to use.

Why processing takes time

The browser processes the audio while the video plays, so the page needs to remain open until the MP3 is ready. A longer video can take longer to process and may use more memory or battery. If you are on a phone, keep the browser in the foreground and avoid switching to a mode that pauses playback before the download finishes.

Troubleshooting MP3 extraction

  • No preview appears. Confirm that you copied a direct link to a public video post. Profile links and photo-only posts do not contain the video to process.
  • The video plays but no sound is present. The post may contain a silent clip or the sound may be very quiet. The extractor cannot create an audio track that is not in the source.
  • Processing pauses. Keep the tab open, check that your device is not suspending the browser, and retry if the page stopped responding.
  • The MP3 does not save. Check browser download permissions, storage space, and the Downloads list. If using an in-app browser, open ThreadsGrab directly in your regular browser.

Save and reuse audio responsibly

An MP3 copy does not change the creator’s rights in the original sound. Ask for permission before reposting, distributing, editing, or using audio in a commercial project. ThreadsGrab does not require a Threads password and does not bypass privacy settings.
