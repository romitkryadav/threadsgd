How to Download Threads Videos: A Step-by-Step Guide

Use a post link from a public Threads video, preview the versions available, then save the file through your browser. This guide covers the steps and common download problems.

Guide by ThreadsGrab · Updated November 20, 2026

Before you download

Start with the exact Threads post that contains the video. A profile link opens the creator’s page, while a post link points to the clip you want to save. In the Threads app, open the post and use its share menu to copy the link. On the website, copy the address of the post itself. The wording and placement of share controls can vary slightly by app version.

The post must be publicly viewable to someone who is not signed in. ThreadsGrab does not sign into your account, ask for your password, or retrieve media from a private or removed post. If the post will not open in a logged-out browser, the downloader may not be able to access it either.

In short: copy a public video post link, paste it into the Threads Video Downloader, preview the available file, then save it through your browser.

Download a Threads video step by step

  1. Open the post, not just the profile. Find the post with the video and copy that post’s link. If you copied a link from a profile, return to the clip and copy its link instead.
  2. Paste the link into ThreadsGrab. Open the Threads Video Downloader, paste the URL into the input box, and select the page’s download or load control. If your browser offers a Paste button, you can use that instead of typing.
  3. Wait for the preview. The tool checks the public post and displays the video it can retrieve. If multiple files or resolutions are available, review the options before saving.
  4. Choose a version. A larger resolution can look clearer on a large screen, but it also uses more storage and data. If you plan to watch on a phone, a smaller available version may be enough. The options depend on the video Threads serves.
  5. Save the file. Select the download control for the version you want and follow the browser prompt. If the browser appears to do nothing, check its downloads list and permissions before repeating the download.

Find the downloaded video on your device

On a computer, open the downloads list in your browser or check its Downloads folder. The location can be changed in browser settings, so the file may not be in the same place on every computer.

On Android, check the browser’s download history and the Downloads folder in your file manager. Some devices keep videos in Downloads rather than displaying them immediately in the gallery. You can move the file into a video folder if you want it to appear with other clips.

On iPhone or iPad, Safari may save the file to Files. Use the share sheet to move a compatible video to Photos if that is where you want to keep it. If you opened the downloader inside another app, open the page directly in Safari when that browser blocks downloads.

Why a Threads video may not download

  • The link is not to the post. Copy the direct link from the post with the video, not the creator profile, feed, or a reply.
  • The post is unavailable. It may have been deleted, restricted, or set so it cannot be viewed publicly. A downloader cannot restore a removed post.
  • The post contains a photo instead. Use the Threads Image Downloader for one photo or the Threads Carousel Downloader for multiple photo slides.
  • The preview works but the file does not save. Check for a browser permission prompt, free storage, and the Downloads folder. Try opening the page in your main browser instead of an in-app browser.

Video quality, format, and attribution

The available file and resolution depend on what Threads makes accessible for that post. A downloader cannot reconstruct details that were not present in the uploaded video, so a larger file is not necessarily a sharper original. Captions, logos, or watermarks already included in the video remain part of it.

Saving a copy does not give you permission to repost, edit, or use the creator’s work commercially. Keep the original context and ask the creator before reusing a video when permission is needed. ThreadsGrab is an independent service and is not affiliated with Threads or Meta.
