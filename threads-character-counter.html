Count Characters in a Threads Post as You Write

Use this Threads character counter to check a draft before you publish it. Type or paste your text to see the character count, characters without spaces, word count, and number of lines. The progress indicator shows how much room remains—or how far your draft is over the target.

Choose a Threads Post or Text Attachment

A standard Threads post has a 500-character limit, according to Meta's launch announcement. Meta later announced text attachments of up to 10,000 characters for longer passages. Availability may depend on the current Threads experience, so confirm the option in your composer. See Meta's Threads launch announcement and its text attachment announcement.

How This Character Counter Counts

The counter uses visible Unicode graphemes when your browser supports them. It counts spaces, punctuation, and line breaks in the main total, and shows a separate count without whitespace. Many emoji joined into a single symbol count as one visible character in modern browsers; older browsers may count some combined symbols differently. Words are separated by whitespace, and each new line is counted separately.

Check the Threads Composer Before Posting

This page is a drafting aid, not an official Threads validator. Threads may process links, line breaks, or combined Unicode characters differently from this counter. Paste the final text into the Threads composer and use its displayed count as the final check.

Keep Your Draft in Your Browser

Character counting happens on this page in your browser. The tool does not connect to your Threads account or publish the text. Use Copy text to move your draft into Threads when you are ready.
