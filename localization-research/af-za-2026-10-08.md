# ThreadsGrab Afrikaans localization research brief
Date: 2026-10-08
Target market/language: South Africa / Afrikaans (af-ZA)

## Search-language observations
- Current web results use the natural Afrikaans noun “aflaaier” for downloader and phrases such as “Threads video aflaaier”.
- “Beeld aflaaier” is used for image downloader intent; “profielprent” / “profielprente” is natural for profile picture; “volgelinge” and “volgelinge teller” are natural for follower-count intent.
- Search results are sparse and mixed-language, so no search-volume or popularity claim is made.
- English product terms such as Threads, MP4, JPEG/PNG, URL and @username should remain where they are clearer or technically necessary.

## Localization decisions
Use concise Afrikaans headings and UI labels, keep ThreadsGrab and technical formats unchanged, avoid literal English syntax, and avoid unsupported claims about speed, unlimited use, HD/4K availability, privacy guarantees, or follower-count accuracy.
