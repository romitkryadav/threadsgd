How to Download Threads Videos, Images & DPs

Three steps, any public Threads post or profile — no account needed.

Copy the link

In the Threads app, tap the share icon under a post and choose Copy link. On a computer, copy the address from your browser's address bar. For a profile picture, copy the link to the profile instead of a post.

Paste it and find the media

Paste the link in the box above and press Find media. ThreadsGrab checks the link and shows what it found: a video, one or more photos, or a profile picture.

Preview and save

Check the preview, choose a quality if there's a choice, then press Download on the file you want. It saves through your browser like any other download, usually to your Downloads folder.

What You Can Save From Threads

ThreadsGrab works with what Threads shows publicly. Here's what each kind of link gives you.

Videos and GIFs

Videos save as MP4 files at the quality Threads served for that post. ThreadsGrab doesn't re-compress them, upscale them, or add a watermark. If the video already has text or a logo on screen, that stays — it's part of the original file.

GIFs on Threads are stored as short video clips, so they save as MP4 too, not as .gif files. For posts with several videos or a choice of qualities, the Threads Video Downloader lays out each option.

Photos and Multi-Photo Posts

Paste the post link and each photo appears as its own item. You save the ones you want. The file is the size Threads provides for that post — a small original stays small. For posts that are mostly photos, the Threads Image Downloader is built around that workflow.

Profile Pictures

Enter a public Threads profile link or type the account's username, with or without the @ symbol. ThreadsGrab retrieves the largest version of the profile picture that Threads makes available, rather than artificially enlarging a smaller image. The actual resolution varies by account. Use the Threads Profile Picture Downloader to preview and save the image.

Text-only Threads posts don't contain any downloadable media, so ThreadsGrab cannot generate a download link for them.

Which Threads Links Work

Copy links straight from Threads. The shapes you'll see:

Link typeLooks likeWhat you get
Post linkthreads.com/share/AbC123The post's video, photos or GIF
Older domainthreads.net/@username/post/AbC123Same — threads.net redirects to threads.com
Profile linkthreads.com/@usernameThe profile picture
Link with extra text…/post/AbC123?…Post media, if the parser handles the additional parameters

What won't work: private accounts, deleted posts, text-only posts, preview cards for external articles, and links to feeds, search or hashtag pages. A link to a reply or quote post points to that reply, not the original — open the original post and copy its link if that's the video you want.

A quick test: open the link in a private or incognito window. If Threads asks you to log in or shows nothing, ThreadsGrab can't get it either.

Saving on iPhone, Android and Computer

iPhone and iPad. Use Safari. Depending on your iOS version you'll get a Save Video or Save to Photos prompt, or the file will appear in Files → Downloads. From Files you can move a video to Photos.

Android. Files usually go to your Downloads folder, and some browsers also add them to your gallery. Check both if you can't find the file.

Computer. Files go to your browser's default Downloads folder. A long video takes a moment to write to disk.

On a phone, copy the link in the Threads app, switch to your browser, then paste it. Long videos are large files — use Wi-Fi if you're on a limited data plan.
