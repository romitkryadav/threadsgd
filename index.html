Frequently Asked Questions- FAQ

Is ThreadsGrab free and do I need to create an account?

Yes, ThreadsGrab is completely free to use, with no sign-up and no login required at any step. This is a deliberate design choice — no account means we're never storing personal data tied to what you download.

Does ThreadsGrab download Threads videos without a watermark?

Yes. We pull the video file directly from Threads' own servers rather than screen-recording your device, so nothing is overlaid or re-encoded in the process. What you download is the same file the creator originally posted.

Why is the Threads downloader unable to work on some of the links I have posted?

Three things cause this almost every time: the account is private, the post was deleted or edited after you copied the link, or the link points to a profile page instead of the specific post. Use the Share icon on the exact post to copy the correct link.

Why does the downloaded video or image look lower quality?

ThreadsGrab downloads media in the highest quality available from the original Threads post. If a video or image looks lower quality, the original upload may have already been compressed by Threads or uploaded in a lower resolution.

Why can't I download content from private accounts?

ThreadsGrab has no way to authenticate into private accounts, and that's intentional. We only process publicly accessible URLs and never ask for a Threads username or password — any tool that does should be treated as a red flag.

Why does the downloader work on one browser but not another?

Because browsers implement user scripts and privileges differently hence if the downloader did not identify your browser as working it may still be possible that another browser would successfully find the downloader's application and download it or install it properly in that case.

Does ThreadsGrab work on iPhone, Android, and tablets?

Yes — ThreadsGrab runs entirely in your browser, so there's nothing to install on iOS or Android. Copy the link from the Share menu inside the Threads app, then paste it here from your phone's browser.

Is it safe to use ThreadsGrab? Do you store my downloads?

No account is required, we never ask for your Threads login, and downloaded media isn't kept on our servers after your session. See our Privacy Policy for the full breakdown.

Is it legal to download videos and images from Threads?

Saving publicly posted content for personal, offline viewing is generally fine. Reusing or redistributing someone else's content commercially without permission is a separate copyright issue that applies no matter what tool was used to download it — that responsibility sits with the user, not the downloader.
