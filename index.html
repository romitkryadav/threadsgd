What You Can Download Here

ThreadsGrab identifies the type of public Threads content in the link you paste and shows the matching download option.

  • Videos download as the original MP4, at the highest resolution available from the post — no re-encoding. For quality options, multi-video posts, and GIF handling, use the Threads Video Downloader.
  • Photos and carousels save as individual images at their original resolution. For carousel ordering and multi-image posts, use the Threads Image Downloader.
  • Profile pictures come out at full size instead of the small circular preview in the app. For anything specific to profile pictures, use the Threads Profile Picture Downloader.
  • Follower counts aren't something this tool downloads. If that's what you're after, the Threads Follower Counter tracks that instead.

What ThreadsGrab Can't Do

ThreadsGrab only works with content that's publicly visible on Threads — posts and profiles you could open without logging in. It can't reach private accounts, follower-only content, or anything behind a login wall, and it isn't built to get around those restrictions. If you can't open a post in a private browser window while logged out of Threads, ThreadsGrab won't be able to either.

What Happens to Your Link and File

When you paste a link, ThreadsGrab's servers fetch the media tied to that specific post and hand it to your browser to download. The file isn't stored or cached on our servers afterward, and there's no account system, so nothing is tied to your identity in the first place.

If a Link Won't Work

  • The URL looks cut off. A truncated link — common when copying from a mobile share sheet — is the most frequent reason a paste fails to process. Copy it again and check that it's complete.
  • You copied a link to a reply or quote post, not the original. Open the actual post you want and copy that link specifically.
  • The post was deleted or edited since you copied the link. Go back to Threads and copy it fresh.
  • The account or post is private. There's no way around this by design.
  • The link is from threads.net or threads.com but still won't load. Both domains are supported; try pasting it into the box again, since occasionally a copy gets an extra character appended.

Using What You Download Responsibly

Saving a file to your device isn't the same as having the right to repost, redistribute, or reuse it. Download content you created yourself, have explicit permission to use, or otherwise have a legitimate reason to keep a copy of. When in doubt, ask the creator or check Threads' own terms before reusing someone else's content. This isn't legal advice — rules vary by location and use case.
